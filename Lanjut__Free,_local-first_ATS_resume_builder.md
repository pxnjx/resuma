## How it works

The same resume at every stage: what you type, what recruiters see, and what the parser reads back.

01 · Template

### Pick a template

Start from a template you like. Each one only changes typography, spacing, and accents; the structure underneath stays ATS-safe.

The Lanjut template gallery showing the same resume in different typographic styles

02 · Content

### Type it once

Fill structured sections: experience, education, skills. Every change lands in your browser's storage and nowhere else.

The Lanjut editor with a resume preview beside structured sections

03 · Export

### Survive the parser

Download PDF, DOCX, or plain text, and any extractor reads it back in the order you wrote it. That's the whole trick.

what the ATS reads.pdf · .docx · .txt

$ pdftotext resume.pdf -

EXPERIENCE

Senior Frontend Engineer, Acme Corp

Mar 2022 – Present

\- Led migration of a 200k-line codebase to a typed component library, cutting UI defects by 40%
